## Transparent PNG checklist

When requesting transparent assets:
- explicitly ask for true alpha transparency;
- say no checkerboard background;
- say no solid background;
- ask to preserve text, shadows, lines, glows, and graphical elements;
- verify output by placing over a dark and light background.
